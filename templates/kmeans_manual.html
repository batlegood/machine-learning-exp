Exercise Manual K-Means

Explanation of the Exercise

The topic is the segmentation of students according to their study hours and academic performance. A dataset of 100 students was used with two numerical variables: study hours per week and average grade out of 100.

Since 3 clusters were defined, three initial centroids were selected: C1=(3,45), C2=(8,65) and C3=(14,85). Each centroid represents an initial group in the plane.

To assign each student to a cluster, the Euclidean distance between the student and each centroid was calculated: 

d = √((X - Xc)² + (Y - Yc)²)

Example: Student 1 (2,45) has distances of 1 to C1, 20.88 to C2 and 41.76 to C3. Since the minimum distance is 1, the student is assigned to Cluster 1.

In Excel, the RAIZ and PROMEDIO.SI formulas allow automatically calculating the distances and new centroids. For example, the Cluster column uses: 

=SI(D2=MIN(D2:F2);"C1";SI(E2=MIN(D2:F2);"C2";"C3"))

After assigning the 100 students, the centroids are recalculated as the average of study hours and grades for each group. These new centroids will be used in the Iteration 2.

In conclusion, the Iteration 1 consists of: starting with initial centroids, calculating distances, assigning students to clusters and recalculating centroids. This process is repeated until the centroids stabilize.
